# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the "Project Plan Quick Editor" Chrome extension
(referred to below as "the extension"). Your privacy matters to us. Please read
this policy to understand how the extension handles your data.

## 1. Our commitment

The extension is a **fully offline** tool. It:

- does **not collect** any personally identifiable information;
- does **not connect** to any server and does **not upload** any data;
- contains **no** ads, analytics, tracking SDKs or third-party scripts;
- does **not read or modify** other websites or tabs you browse.

## 2. What data we collect

**We do not collect any user data.**

The project names, work items, milestones, tasks, text formatting, layout
settings and saved plan snapshots you create in the extension are stored
**only in your own browser's local storage (localStorage)**. They are protected
by the browser's same-origin policy and can only be accessed by the extension
itself.

This data:

- is never sent to us or to any third party;
- never leaves your device;
- can be permanently deleted at any time by clearing your browser site data or
  uninstalling the extension.

## 3. Permissions

The extension requests only one permission:

- **clipboardWrite**: used only when you click "Copy Markdown" to copy your own
  exported content to the system clipboard. The extension never reads or
  modifies the clipboard in the background.

The extension does **not** request browsing history, tabs, cookies, location,
camera, microphone or storage sync permissions.

## 4. Third-party services and data sharing

The extension **uses no third-party services**, loads no external scripts,
fonts, images or network resources, and does **not sell, rent or share** any
data with third parties.

## 5. Data security

Because your data is stored only in your local browser, its security depends on
your browser's local security mechanisms. We recommend that you:

- regularly back up important plans using the "Export Markdown" feature;
- avoid keeping sensitive project information on untrusted shared devices for
  extended periods.

## 6. Children's privacy

The extension does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If we change how the extension handles data in the future (for example, by
adding an optional sync feature), we will update this policy and mark the new
effective date. Material changes will be highlighted prominently.
